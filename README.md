# 🌍 ShopImpact – Conscious Shopping Dashboard

## Project Overview

ShopImpact is an interactive Python-based web application designed to promote **conscious consumerism**. The app allows users to log their shopping purchases and instantly view the estimated environmental (CO₂) impact of their spending. By visualising monthly impact, awarding eco badges, and suggesting greener alternatives, ShopImpact encourages users to reflect on their habits and make more sustainable choices.

This project is developed as part of the **CRS – Artificial Intelligence | Python Programming Summative Assessment**, aligned with *Scenario 1: Build ShopImpact Conscious Shopping Dashboard using Python*.

---

## Purpose & Social Impact

Many consumers are unaware of the hidden environmental cost of everyday purchases. ShopImpact addresses this problem by:

* Increasing awareness of carbon footprint
* Encouraging eco-friendly decision-making
* Making sustainability engaging rather than guilt-driven

The app is targeted at **students, young adults, and eco-conscious families** who want to track and improve their shopping behaviour.

---

## Key Features

### 🛒 Purchase Logging

* Input product type (Clothing, Electronics, Groceries, Footwear, Second-hand Items)
* Enter brand name and price
* Purchases are stored using Python lists and dictionaries

### 🌱 CO₂ Impact Calculation

* Each product category has a predefined impact multiplier
* Environmental impact is calculated in real time using:

  `Impact = Price × Category Multiplier`

### 📊 Monthly Impact Dashboard

* Displays total monthly spending
* Shows estimated total CO₂ impact
* Visual bar chart for easy comparison

### 🏅 Eco Badge & Alerts

* Users receive eco alerts when their impact is high
* Motivational messages encourage greener habits

### ♻️ Greener Alternatives

* Suggested eco-friendly options for each product type
* Example: Organic brands, thrift stores, refurbished electronics

### 💡 Eco Tips

* Random eco-friendly tips displayed after logging a purchase
